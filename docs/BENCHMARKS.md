# Benchmarks

What this repository measured against what experts annotated, on the same images and on the same terms. **This page is an index**: one section per benchmark, each with what it asks, one row per model, and links to the pages that argue it. Nothing here is the argument — the per-dataset detail, the caveats and the reasoning live behind those links, and `results/` holds the score of every photograph behind every number. It is generated by the runs.

**Read a row, not a column.** A result marked `in-sample` or `unknown` is not comparable with one marked `out-of-sample`, and a model agreeing with another model is not evidence that either agrees with an expert. Every figure below is **pooled over the datasets that benchmark measured**, which the section says; a model is rarely equally good on all of them, and where it is not, that is on its results page rather than here.

## Disc

**Where exactly are the optic disc and the cup inside it?** Each model's outline is compared with the one an ophthalmologist drew on the same photograph — every ophthalmologist separately, never an averaged consensus — and with the cup-to-disc ratio computed from it, which is the number a glaucoma referral rests on.

[How it is run](benchmarks/disc-docs.md) · [What came out](benchmarks/disc-results.md) · [The analysis](../notebooks/disc.ipynb) · [Every score](../results/disc/)

Pooled over **chaksu, grape, papila**, one comparison per reader. **Dice** is overlap with the expert's outline, 0 to 1. **Ratio error** is signed: positive means the model reads the cup-to-disc ratio *higher* than the ophthalmologist did, which is the direction that sends people to a clinic. A dash means the model does not find that structure at all.

**Where to start.**

- **For the disc alone**: **vascx-disc** (Dice 0.951). **segformer-disc-cup** is 0.014 behind (0.937), which is inside the disagreement between two ophthalmologists — the benchmark cannot separate them.
- **For a cup-to-disc ratio**, which is what leaves the building: **lunetv2-odc**, whose ratio sits -0.001 from the ophthalmologists' on average, against -0.031 for automorph-disc-cup.
- **vascx-disc finds no cup at all**, so it cannot produce that ratio at any score. The two rows above answer different questions and a reader needs to know which one they are asking.

A pooled bias near zero can be a model reading high on one dataset and low on another — [what came out](benchmarks/disc-results.md) breaks it apart, and holds the readers' own disagreement, which is the ceiling any of this is read against.

## Quality

**Is this photograph good enough to measure?** Every quality model here is asked that of the same photographs, and its answer is compared with the grade the dataset's own readers wrote down. A gate that throws away usable photographs costs a study its sample; one that lets bad photographs through costs it every biomarker computed from them.

[How it is run](benchmarks/quality-docs.md) · [What came out](benchmarks/quality-results.md) · [The analysis](../notebooks/quality.ipynb) · [Every score](../results/quality/)

Measured on **fives, fqs, mshf, papila**. **Accuracy and κ are read together**: accuracy flatters a model on a dataset where one class dominates, and Cohen's κ is what is left after chance agreement is taken out. Where they disagree, believe κ.

The pooled figures cover **fives, fqs, mshf**, whose readers used more than one grade, and the Photographs column counts those. papila is left out of them: every photograph in it is assumed sound, so there is nothing to be right about — what a model does there is a keep-rate, in its own column.

Each figure is computed over all those photographs **at once** rather than averaged over the datasets, which is the harder test: a model whose scores mean different things on different cameras loses here and not there.

**Where to start.**

- **As it ships**, agreeing with the readers at its own published threshold: **quickqual** (κ 0.671). **vascx-quality** is 0.004 behind (κ 0.666), which is close enough that the choice is about which mistake you would rather make.
- **At ordering photographs**, which is what matters if you will set your own threshold rather than take the one it ships with: **vascx-quality** (ROC AUC 0.942), with **fit-quality** at 0.940 — too close to order by.
- **Those are different models, and that is the finding.** vascx-quality separates good photographs from bad ones better than anything else here and then gates in the wrong place: it keeps 96% of the photographs the readers called worth measuring.

Read [what came out](benchmarks/quality-results.md) before acting on this: coverage, contamination and the threshold each model happens to ship with all differ, and the caveats are there rather than here.
