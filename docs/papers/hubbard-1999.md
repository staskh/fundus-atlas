# Hubbard 1999

The paper that turned vessel widths around the optic disc into a single arteriolar and venular
number, and into their ratio, for a large population study. Every catalogued pipeline that reports
CRAE, CRVE or AVR is implementing this protocol or Knudtson's later revision of it.

## 2. What it is about

The Atherosclerosis Risk in Communities (ARIC) study needed a repeatable way to judge retinal
vessels on photographs taken without dilating the pupil. Graders looked at slides for focal
narrowing, arteriovenous nicking and retinopathy, and they measured the widths of vessels crossing a
zone around the optic disc on digitised images. Those widths were then combined, pair by pair, into
a central arteriolar equivalent and a central venular equivalent, and the two were divided to give
an artery-to-vein ratio. The paper is the protocol, the formulas, and the first large-study
reproducibility figures for that ratio.

## 3. Why it is in this atlas

- **Kind:** definition
- It is one of the two definitions of record for [central retinal equivalents](../biomarkers/central-retinal-equivalents.md)
  and therefore for [AVR](../biomarkers/avr.md). The pairing rule, the zone around the disc, and
  the additive constants that only make sense in microns all start here. Knudtson later revised the
  formulas; this page is the original.

## 4. What the authors claim

| Claim | Sample | Reported in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photographs were obtained in 99% of participants and were of sufficient quality to grade in 81% | ARIC visit 3, four centres | this paper |
| Artery-to-vein ratio ranged from 0.57 to 1.22 (median 0.84) | 9,040 subjects with usable photographs, diabetes excluded from this analysis | this paper |
| Replicate grading of the ratio: correlation 0.79, median absolute difference 0.03 | Masked replicate sample | this paper |
| Several microvascular changes were associated with higher blood pressure | Same ARIC sample, adjusted for age, sex, race and smoking | this paper |

These are the authors' measurements on ARIC photographs. This atlas's own comparisons are separate.

## 6. Notes

- The additive constants were fitted in **microns**. Feeding pixel widths into the Hubbard formulas
  is not a rescaled Hubbard value; it is a different number. That trap is recorded on the
  [equivalents](../biomarkers/central-retinal-equivalents.md) page, not as a defect in this paper.
- Parr and Spears's earlier branching work is the source Hubbard fitted; it is not catalogued here
  yet, because the operational definition the field used is this one.
