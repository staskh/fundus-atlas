# Hart 1999

The paper that named seven ways to score how twisted a retinal vessel is, and showed they are not
rescalings of one another. Every catalogued pipeline that reports "tortuosity" is picking a subset
of this list — and none of them picks the pair the paper's compositionality argument favours.

## 2. What it is about

Tortuosity is a clinician's word. Hart and colleagues turned it into seven formulas on a traced
vessel — from the simple ratio of path length to straight-line length, through integrals of
curvature and of squared curvature, each with or without dividing by length — and they asked which
of those formulas keep the properties a clinician's judgement seems to have: that a uniformly
scaled photograph should not change the ranking, and that the score of a whole vessel should be
recoverable from the scores of its parts. They then used the measures to classify vessel segments
and whole networks against ophthalmologists' labels.

## 3. Why it is in this atlas

- **Kind:** definition
- It is one of the two definitions of record for [tortuosity](../biomarkers/tortuosity.md). The
  table of τ1–τ7, their scale response, and which of them are compositional all start here. Without
  that table, two pipelines reporting "tortuosity" look as if they are reporting the same number.

## 4. What the authors claim

| Claim | Sample | Reported in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The suite of measures classified vessel segments at about 91% and vessel networks at about 95% against ophthalmologists' notion of tortuosity | Vessel segments extracted from colour fundus photographs, some manually and some automatically | this paper |
| No single measure was clearly superior; the classification experiments recommend a total squared curvature measure | Same classification tasks | this paper |
| How the centreline is extracted changes the scores, because a vessel at 45° to the pixel grid is stored as a zigzag | Authors' discussion of automatic versus manual traces | this paper |

These are the authors' measurements and arguments. This atlas's own comparisons are separate.

## 6. Notes

- This atlas's [tortuosity](../biomarkers/tortuosity.md) page treats τ4 and τ5 — mean curvature and
  mean squared curvature, the compositional pair — as the measures matching Hart's argument about
  combining parts, and records that **no catalogued pipeline implements them**. AutoMorph reports
  τ3, which Hart's table marks non-compositional. That is this atlas's reading of the paper against
  the code, not a claim the paper makes about later software.
- The centreline must be smoothed before curvature is estimated. Hart is explicit about this; an
  implementation that skips it is not computing Hart's κ.
