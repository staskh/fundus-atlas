# The disc-and-cup benchmark's two documents

Load this beside `SKILL.md` when changing `docs/benchmarks/disc-docs.md` or
`docs/benchmarks/disc-results.md`.

## 1. What `disc-docs.md` must explain that no other benchmark has

Generated by the run:

- **The resampling path**, per model: whether its output arrived as probabilities and was
  interpolated to native before thresholding, or arrived binary and was resampled nearest. It moves
  every metric on the page and a reader cannot infer it.
- **Which structures each model emits.** A disc-only model is absent from the cup tables, and the
  page says absent rather than leaving a blank to be read as zero.
- **How many readers each dataset publishes**, because that decides whether a ceiling can be
  computed at all.
- **Every column of the evidence**, including the reader and structure columns that make this
  benchmark's rows longer than the quality benchmark's.
- **What each metric is**, in one line each, for a reader who knows what an optic disc is and not
  what a Dice coefficient is.

## 2. What `disc-results.md` says, section by section

Written from `notebooks/disc.ipynb`, following `SKILL.md` §4:

1. **Contents.**
2. **Summary — every dataset pooled**, one row per model: photographs, coverage, disc Dice, cup
   Dice, cup-to-disc ratio error, and the contamination mark. Six columns, no more.
3. **Where the boundary sits** — centre offset in disc diameters, and the width and height biases.
   This is the section that separates models with equal Dice.
4. **The cup-to-disc ratio**, with its bias, its spread, and how many photographs cross a referral
   threshold because of the model rather than because of the eye.
5. **Dataset by dataset**, and per reader where a dataset publishes more than one.
6. **Against the readers' own disagreement** — the ceiling, and which models are inside it.
7. **Which model to use**, per `SKILL.md` §5, naming the question: overlap, centring, or the ratio.
8. **What these numbers do not say.**

## 3. The conclusions this benchmark has to state plainly

- **Dice is not the result.** Say which model is best at the measurement anyone acts on, not only at
  overlap, and say where the two disagree.
- **A signed bias is worse than scatter of the same size**, because it moves every patient the same
  way. Name it where it exists.
- **A model inside the readers' own disagreement has not been shown to be better or worse than a
  reader** — and on a dataset with one reader, no such statement is available at all.
- **Where a dataset publishes its own ratio and the contours disagree with it**, that is a finding
  about the dataset, and it belongs on the dataset's page as well as here.
