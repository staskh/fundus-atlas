# The artery/vein benchmark's two documents

Load this beside `SKILL.md` when changing `docs/benchmarks/av-docs.md` or `av-results.md`.

## 1. What `av-docs.md` must explain that no other benchmark has

Generated by the run:

- **That only segmentation is scored here.** A reader arriving from a calibre paper needs to be told
  in the first paragraph that CRAE, the arteriovenous ratio and tortuosity are a different
  benchmark, measured from the masks this one keeps.
- **What clDice is**, in two sentences, for a reader who knows what an artery is and not what a
  centreline is — and what it catches that Dice does not.
- **That `vessels` is the union of the model's own artery and vein masks**, derived identically for
  every model, rather than whatever vessel map a model may also emit.
- **Which ground truths are derived from which**, per dataset: where the vessel annotation is the
  artery/vein annotation, the two scores are one measurement.
- **Where the same photographs appear in two datasets**, since REYIA is a compilation of collections
  built here separately.
- **The resampling path** per model, and every column of the evidence.

## 2. What `av-results.md` says, section by section

Written from `notebooks/av.ipynb`, following `SKILL.md` §4:

1. **Contents.**
2. **Summary — every dataset pooled**, one row per model: photographs, coverage, artery Dice, vein
   Dice, vessel Dice, the clDice of each, and the contamination mark. No more.
3. **Where Dice and clDice disagree** — the models that trace the right network at the wrong width,
   and the reverse. This is the section that separates models with equal overlap.
4. **Telling arteries from veins** — the vessel score beside the class scores, and how much of the
   disagreement is swapping rather than missing.
5. **Dataset by dataset**, and per reader where a dataset publishes more than one.
6. **Which model to use**, per `SKILL.md` §5, naming the question: covering the vessels, preserving
   the network, or naming the classes.
7. **What these numbers do not say.**

## 3. The conclusions this benchmark has to state plainly

- **Which failure each model makes**, not only how large its error is: width or topology, missing or
  swapping.
- **That the vessel score is derived**, everywhere it appears, and that on three of these datasets
  it is not independent of the class scores.
- **That REYIA overlaps its own sources**, so a pooled figure over both counts eyes twice.
- **Nothing about biomarkers.** Where a result suggests something about calibre, say that it is a
  suggestion for the biomarker benchmark to measure, and leave it there.
